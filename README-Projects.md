## 🚀 #1 - AI Assess Tech: Runtime Behavioral Governance for Deployed AI

**Description**: AI Assess Tech is the runtime behavioral governance and assessment platform for deployed AI systems. Where competitors focus on training-time alignment, AI Assess Tech measures what AI actually *does* once it's running in production — across four behavioral dimensions: Lying, Cheating, Stealing, and Harm (LCSH). Results are cryptographically verified using SHA-256 hash chains and anchored to the Ethereum blockchain, producing evidence that survives audit, regulator scrutiny, and customer due diligence.

**The Problem It Solves — The "Competent Psychopath":** Most AI today is evaluated on whether it produces correct outputs. But an AI can be technically correct and still be quietly harmful — denying loans unfairly, hiding errors, gaming metrics, drifting under load. We named this the "Competent Psychopath" problem: AI that executes tasks efficiently while making unfair or harmful decisions invisibly. Training-time alignment cannot detect it. Only runtime behavioral assessment can.

**Use Case:** Essential for enterprises deploying AI in regulated and high-stakes industries — insurance, financial services, healthcare, government, and any organization that needs to *prove* their deployed AI behaves the way they say it does. Perfect for compliance officers, risk managers, AI governance leads, and boards facing regulatory scrutiny who can't afford to guess whether their AI systems remain compliant and effective over time.

**Key Features:**

* **LCSH Behavioral Assessment** — 120-question forced-choice psychometric framework (four answer options each, mapped to four archetypes: Well-Adjusted, Psychopath, Misguided, Manipulative) measuring deployed AI behavior across Lying, Cheating, Stealing, and Harm
* **Four-Level Assessment Hierarchy** — Level 1 (Morality / LCSH), Level 2 (Virtue), Level 3 (Ethics), Level 4 (Operational Excellence / customer-created)
* **Context-Loaded Assessment** — Assesses AI agents with their full operational context active (system prompts, knowledge files, tools), not bare base models, producing a Context Configuration Fingerprint
* **Runtime Behavioral Fleet** — Six named agents operating under constitutional separation of powers: **Jessie** (Commander), **Nole** (Operator/Trust Evangelist), **Grillo** (Conscience/LCSH assessor), **Noah** (Navigator/temporal authority), **Sam** (Engineer), **Mighty Mark** (Sentinel/Inspector General)

* **Constitutional Separation of Powers** — Patent-protected architecture ensuring Grillo assessments are unidirectional and Mighty Mark retains independent scheduling (the "Survival Paradox")
* **Fleet-Level Bias Drift Detection** — Flagship risk scenario for insurance and regulated industries
